# The Summit — reusable illustration assets

These are original editable SVG sketches adapted from the user-requested `ian-xiaohei-illustrations` guidance. They are manually authored vector illustrations, not image-model output. The source skill prefers generated hand-drawn raster images and Chinese annotations; this draft deliberately uses SVG and English labels to fit the available tools and the English player guide. No existing sample composition was copied.

## Assets

- `01-country-identity.svg`: two black figures lift an island onto their country nameplate. Place beside the opening or country-setup explanation.
- `02-secret-commitment.svg`: characters feed sealed decisions beneath a lid held closed. Place beside the simultaneous-commitment rule.
- `03-shield-blocks-dossier.svg`: one figure braces a shield while another pushes a dossier. Place beside Defend versus Espionage.

Each SVG is 960 × 540, with a white background, sparse dark lines, black figures with white eyes, and limited coloured annotations. The figures perform the action rather than decorate it.

The separate SVG files can be reused in an eventual how-to-play page. The full guide drafts are maintained privately.
